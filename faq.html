Questions, answered

Q: How does the free trial work?

You can try Aperto free for 15 days. Choose a plan on the pricing page to begin.

Q: Who is Aperto for?

Aperto is made for wedding and christening photographers, and works well for any photographer who takes bookings for events.

Q: How does Aperto work?

Aperto is hosted for you. After you sign up you get your own private space on our website, and you log in from any browser. Nothing to install, and your data is kept separate from every other customer.

Q: Where is my client data kept?

In your own separate space on the Aperto website. You can download a backup file at any time, and back up to Google Drive or Dropbox.

Q: Can I use Aperto in Greek?

Yes. Aperto works in English and Greek.

Q: Which apps can I connect?

More than 30, including Google Calendar, Stripe, PayPal, Telegram, WhatsApp Business, Zoom, Dropbox and Google Drive. You can also export for Xero and QuickBooks. See the integrations page.

Q: Do I need to be technical to use it?

No. Aperto is designed to be calm and simple. If you get stuck there is a help panel inside the app, and you can email us.

Q: Can I cancel at any time?

Yes. You can cancel whenever you like. Final terms will be on the terms page.

Q: How do I get help?

Write to general@apertocrm.com or use the contact page.
